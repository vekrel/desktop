Run coding agents on your own machines, around the clock.

Vekrel runs Codex, Claude and other coding agents on computers you own, and lets you check on them and steer them from a browser or your phone.

Your code and your agents stay on your machine. The Vekrel relay only handles sign-in and tells your devices how to reach each other.
