Privacy policy

Last updated 27 October 2026

This page covers the hosted Vekrel service: the relay at relay.vekrel.com and the web app at rc.vekrel.com. The Vekrel software you run on your own machines is yours to operate; what it does with your data is up to you and the agent providers you connect it to.

What we store

  • Your account: email address, and the GitHub or Google account ID you signed in with.
  • Sign-in records: short-lived sign-in codes and links, and session tokens (stored as hashes).
  • Your linked machines: name, public key and the address your devices use to reach them.
  • If you use the mobile app: device type, OS version, notification settings and push tokens.

What we don't store

Your code, prompts, files and agent output. Traffic between your devices and your machines goes through a Cloudflare Tunnel straight to your machine; the relay doesn't receive it.

Who processes it

  • Railway hosts the relay and its database.
  • Cloudflare serves the web app and runs the tunnels to your machines.
  • Resend sends sign-in emails.
  • GitHub and Google, if you sign in with them.
  • Apple and Google push services, if you turn on mobile notifications.

We don't sell your data or use it for advertising. Short-lived sign-in data expires automatically.

Deleting your data

Email info@vekrel.com from your account's address and we'll delete your account and everything linked to it.
